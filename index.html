About the App

An Independent App, Designed to Be Lightweight and Clear

Map Explorer is an independent map app and is not affiliated with Apple or Google. It uses available map data to display your location accurately and help you navigate with ease.

Saved location data remains on your device only and is not intentionally sent to any external server — your privacy is at the heart of the app’s design.
